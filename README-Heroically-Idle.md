# Heroically Idle

**One tiny hero. Thirteen ridiculous realms. An adventure that keeps going even when you leave.**

Heroically Idle is a humorous fantasy idle RPG that combines an endless runner, automatic combat, equipment collecting, moral choices and an evolving LitRPG story. Watch your pixel-art hero travel through absurd fantasy kingdoms, battle unlikely enemies, acquire increasingly powerful equipment and become entangled in a mystery surrounding the very nature of their adventure.

Unlike a traditional RPG, your hero continues adventuring without constant input. Jump over enemies, collect coins, make decisions, purchase upgrades or simply watch the story unfold. Return after time away to discover what your hero accomplished in your absence.

## Core Gameplay

Your hero automatically runs through the current realm, collecting coins and encountering enemies. Tap the runway to jump, dodge hazards and collect airborne coins. Combat occurs automatically when enemies are encountered.

Spend earned coins in the shop to strengthen your hero, improve movement and increase income. Each realm contains 300 named levels, divided into ten stages apiece. Boss encounters conclude levels, and defeating a realm's final boss opens the next kingdom.

The live story feed narrates combat, discoveries, conversations, side quests, loot, moral dilemmas and sarcastic announcements from the mysterious System. Players can actively participate or let the adventure progress on its own.

Five speed settings — real-time, ×4, ×20, ×100 and ×400 — allow players to choose their preferred active pace.

## Make It Yours

Customize your hero's name, pronouns, catchphrase, pet name, nemesis name and a friend's name. These optional details appear throughout the narrative, with the named friend potentially appearing as a companion, shopkeeper or quest character.

Every new hero also receives a randomized **Story DNA**, consisting of a title, origin story, flaw, obsession, catchphrase, fear, patron organization, lifelong pet, nemesis grudge and secret. These traits recur throughout the adventure and may change through major plot twists.

The **Legend Card** creates a shareable portrait of your hero featuring their appearance, equipment, pet, title, level, realm, personality traits, accomplishments and distinctive Legend number.

## Moral Choices and Alignment

Recurring dilemmas offer three responses: **Good**, **Neutral** and **Evil**. Decisions change narrative outcomes, rewards, consequences and alignment. Alignment influences future encounters and certain benefits, including chest discounts for evil heroes. Unanswered dilemmas resolve automatically according to the hero's alignment.

## Combat and Equipment

Automatic battles scale with hero level, equipment power and companion bonuses. Ordinary levels conclude with champions, every tenth level features a guardian or realm boss, and level 300 concludes with the realm's unique boss. Enemy hits cost coins rather than health, allowing the adventure to continue without traditional game-over interruptions. Combat momentum can increase travel pace.

Collect equipment for ten slots: helm, amulet, ring, tunic, shield, weapon, gloves, leggings, boots and charm. Gear comes in Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary rarities, with humorous names and varying power. Stronger items automatically equip, while weaker items are sold. Higher-rarity equipment adds visual flourishes to the hero.

Four treasure chest tiers offer additional gear: Suspiciously Damp Chest, Iron-ish Chest, Gilded Chest of Bad Ideas and Mythic Lockbox (Probably Cursed).

## Upgrades, Fame and Legacy

The shop offers 18 upgrade types covering coin value and collection, jump height, double jumping, automatic hopping, sword speed, momentum, chest luck, boss damage, offline income, companion power, fame bonuses, XP progression and reduced coin losses.

From realm three, Golden Ledger, Training Fund and Guild Banner provide extended progression, each with up to 500 upgrade levels. The Legacy shop lets players commission bards to begin side quests, erect statues granting permanent coin bonuses, and sponsor festivals that temporarily double coin value. Fans earned through adventures, quests and celebrations can further strengthen income.

## Companions and Pets

Up to three temporary companions can travel with your hero, adding combat power and appearing in the story. Their progress-based stays conclude with farewell messages. Possible companions include an embarrassed dragon hatchling, an orc accountant and a raccoon wearing a cape.

A lifelong pet accompanies the hero on the runway, has its own pixel-art sprite and participates in recurring narrative events. Some major story twists can reveal unexpected truths about the pet or other characters.

## An Evolving LitRPG Story

The main narrative follows a mysterious correspondent known only as **F**, whose unusual messages lead the hero into a larger mystery involving previous Chosen Ones, questionable prophecies and the nature of the world itself. The Grand Plot unfolds through four story beats per realm, with flashbacks and additional New Game+ introductions.

Alongside the main plot are 39 realm-specific quests, 29 general quests, 12 month-themed calendar quests and procedurally assembled multi-part adventures. Examples include The Great Hedge Dispute, The Owl With The Questions, The Accidental Wedding and The Dragon's Therapist.

Twenty-eight once-per-save twists can change the hero's identity or world. Completed adventures leave memories that later scenes may reference. The story combines authored scenes, personalized text, realm-specific events and generated dialogue, with recent-repeat suppression to keep the feed varied. Its humor draws on absurd fantasy, self-aware LitRPG narration and ridiculous everyday problems in extraordinary settings.

## Offline Adventure and Automation

Your hero continues progressing while you are away. Returning after at least 20 seconds triggers an offline recap covering up to 48 hours of absence. Offline progression can award coins, XP and loot, advance levels or realms, and continue story events at a slower pace than active play. Missed main plot beats can be delivered as flashbacks.

Optional Shop and Chest Gnomes automatically purchase upgrades and open selected treasure chests within spending limits, including during offline progression.

## Collections, Rewards and Replayability

- **Trophies:** More than 50 achievements with coin rewards for exploration, combat, wealth, quests, fame and other milestones.
- **Bestiary:** Discover and defeat the three base enemies of each realm to fill the collection.
- **Legendary Codex:** Record discovered Legendary equipment.
- **Errands:** Send your hero on timed assignments for coins and gear.
- **Daily gifts:** Claim coin rewards with a consecutive-day multiplier capped at seven days.
- **New Game+:** Complete all thirteen realms to return to Meadowshire with stronger enemies, richer rewards and additional story introductions.

The campaign is designed for long-term play, with continued progression through repeated realm circuits, upgrades, collections and personal stories. Completion time varies substantially with active play, speed settings and offline progression.

## Visuals, Sound and Controls

Colorful pixel-art scenery changes with each realm. The animated hero, visible equipment flourishes, companions, pet, enemies, coins and environmental effects occupy the upper runway, while the lower feed tells the evolving story. Synthesized sound effects can be toggled, and text size can be adjusted for reading comfort.

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Jump | Tap the runway/top half of the screen |
| Double jump | Tap again in midair after unlocking the upgrade |
| Moral choice | Tap Good, Neutral or Evil when available |
| Buy upgrades or chests | Open SHOP |
| View character and Legend Card | Open Profile |
| Collections, errands, automation | Open the menu |
| Change active pace | Tap the speed control |

## Saving and Availability

Designed primarily for portrait mobile play, Heroically Idle also supports desktop and landscape browser layouts. Progress automatically saves locally, and copyable backup codes allow a hero to be transferred between devices. The game works without a network connection once loaded and includes no accounts, advertising or tracking. Shop purchases use earned in-game coins.
